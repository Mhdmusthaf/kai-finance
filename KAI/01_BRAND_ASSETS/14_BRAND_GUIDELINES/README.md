**Rules of thumb**
- Use SVG wherever the tool accepts it; PNGs for social, decks, docs.
- 3D mascot PNGs/SVGs are for marketing and product moments. The logo always uses the flat or one-colour mascot.
- The 3D SVGs use blur filters: they open in browsers, Figma and Illustrator; very old tools may flatten them — use the PNGs there.
- Never retype "KAI": the wordmark is outlined Nunito ExtraBold with custom spacing.
- Business card: replace the [bracketed] placeholders before printing.
