Final recommendation The Nunito family — Nunito for the voice, Nunito Sans for the numbers.
One superfamily, one designer’s skeleton. Nunito’s rounded terminals give the wordmark its subtle cuteness; its geometric construction keeps it serious. Nunito Sans shares every proportion with squared terminals and tabular figures, so long-form UI text and ₹ amounts stay crisp at 12 px. Sora, Manrope and DM Sans read too technical or neutral; Plus Jakarta is strong but corporate.
