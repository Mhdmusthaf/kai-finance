# KAI — Your Money OS · Brand + App UI/UX

Everything for KAI in one place:

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `01_BRAND_ASSETS/` | Full brand identity: logos, wordmark, monogram, 3D mascot and 10 expressions, app icons, UI icons, colours, typography and fonts, backgrounds, patterns, social, merch, brand guidelines PDF. Files come as SVG / PDF / PNG (1024 · 2048 · 4096). Open `KAI_Asset_Library.html` to browse them. |
| `02_APP_UI_UX/` | Mobile app (iOS + Android) design handoff: 152 screen images (every state), 55 flow boards, product spec, design system, data model, voice/AI rules, `theme.ts`, `types.ts`, `schema.sql`, sample data, and the build prompts. |

**Building with an AI IDE (Antigravity, Cursor, Replit…)**
1. Open this folder.
2. Read `AGENTS.md`.
3. Follow `02_APP_UI_UX/BUILD_PROMPTS.md` phase by phase.

> **About this pack:** it keeps every brand master in vector form (SVG/PDF), the fonts, the app-ready PNGs (in `02_APP_UI_UX/assets/`) and the full app UI/UX.
> Large print PNGs (2048/4096) and the social/merch mockup PNGs are left out to keep this a single download. Any PNG size can be exported from the SVG masters.
